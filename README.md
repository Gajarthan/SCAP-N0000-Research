## Evidence key
- **FACT / ISSUER:** Specific claim from a dated corporate statement. Issuer statements are not independent verification.
- **SECONDARY:** Provider or press claim awaiting primary-source reconciliation.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** A proposition to test, not an established advantage or conclusion.
- **OPEN:** Material data or verification missing.
