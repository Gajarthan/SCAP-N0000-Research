# 03 — Competition

**Status: comparison framework; not a definitive market-share ranking.**

Compare SCAP's subsidiaries with **the relevant business lines**, not SCAP's holding-company ticker versus a standalone insurer or lender.

| SCAP business | Potential comparison set (research candidates) | Comparable measures |
|---|---|---|
| Life insurance | Other licensed life insurers in Sri Lanka, including Ceylinco Life, AIA and HNB Assurance | Growth in premiums, persistency, protection mix, claims, solvency, distributable profits |
| Non-bank finance | Licensed finance companies and leasing providers, e.g. LB Finance and People's Leasing | Loan quality, funding cost, net interest margin, credit losses, liquidity/capital |
| Stockbroking | Licensed CSE member firms | Client flow, research/distribution capabilities, brokerage turnover, fees |
| Asset management | Licensed unit-trust managers | Assets under management, net inflows, investment performance versus benchmarks, fee take-rate |

**Before publishing a competitor metric:** confirm the candidate operated in the same market during the same reporting period; retrieve its report; normalize accounting definitions; and identify whether results are consolidated, standalone or segment-level.

## Potential competitive forces to test

1. Price and fee competition: insurance pricing, loan/deposit rates, trading commissions, fund fees.
2. Distribution: bank referrals, tied agents, branches, digital onboarding, broker relationships.
3. Customer retention: policy renewals, finance refinancing, investment fund churn.
4. Regulation: licensing, solvency, capital, consumer protection, market conduct.
5. Switching costs: real measured persistency/retention, not general claims about loyalty.

## Research tasks

- [ ] Obtain regulator-published list of currently licensed insurers and finance companies.
- [ ] Choose 3–5 comparable firms *per segment* based on a documented criterion.
- [ ] Pull three comparable reporting periods from each company.
- [ ] Normalize definitions and build a side-by-side metric table.
