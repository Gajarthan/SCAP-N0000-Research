# Guide — Research standards

## Classification

- **FACT — issuer**: a specific dated assertion directly supported by an issuer filing or website, correctly scoped and not presumed independent.
- **FACT — regulator**: a specific published regulatory action or statistic with its date and authority.
- **SECONDARY**: news, broker, market-data site, or data aggregator not reconciled to original documents.
- **HYPOTHESIS**: a thesis, possible cause, projected effect or alleged advantage requiring testing.
- **OPEN**: unanswered material question.

## Source hierarchy

Use CSE original statements and corporate announcements, regulator publications and audited reports first. Use reputable third-party providers for discovery and cross-checks, not as substitutes for the original report. Retain the exact URL and reporting period.

## Publishing requirements

- Name the issuer, share class, and the date to which a figure applies.
- Record LKR versus LKR million/billion and consolidated versus parent-company scope.
- Cite the page/note, distinguish observations from interpretation, and document contrary evidence.
- Never present a current market price, stake, regulation or management identity from undated evidence.
- Clearly label an unreconciled financial table and never calculate a target price from it.
- Document scenario assumptions, sensitivity, liquidity and potential for irreversible loss.
- Do not expose personal financial records in public version control.
- No GitHub Actions or automation workflows are required for this research repository.

## Review protocol

When evidence changes, update the relevant document **and** [../RESEARCH-LOG.md](../RESEARCH-LOG.md) with the new date, report source, revised conclusion, and what earlier observation was superseded.
