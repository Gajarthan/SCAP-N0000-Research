# Guide — Reading SCAP's financial statements

SCAP is a financial-services holding company. General manufacturing-company heuristics may be misleading because insurance liabilities, loans and customer deposits are central operating items.

## Reading order

1. **Auditor's report:** Opinion, material uncertainty and key audit matters.
2. **Group structure/ownership note:** Direct/indirect stakes and non-controlling interests.
3. **Consolidated income statement:** Revenue, underwriting/investment/credit costs and profit attributable to parent.
4. **Company-only income statement and balance sheet:** Parent income, borrowings, security, dividends and intercompany balances.
5. **Consolidated balance sheet:** Finance receivables, insurance-contract assets/liabilities, borrowings, deposits, liquidity and investments.
6. **Cash-flow statement:** Operating, investing and financing cash; financial-firm classification differences.
7. **Segment note:** Profit and assets by insurance, finance and other segments with elimination adjustments.
8. **Capital and contingencies:** Finance regulatory capital, insurer solvency, guarantees, pledges and commitments.
9. **Share disclosures:** EPS denominator, issuance, conversion, dilution and dividends.

## Never mix these without reconciliation

- **Gross written premium** vs SLFRS 17 insurance revenue.
- **Group profit** vs profit attributable to **ordinary shareholders of SCAP**.
- **Consolidated debt** vs SCAP holding-company indebtedness.
- **Insurance investment assets** vs freely distributable cash.
- **Calendar-year insurer results** vs **April–March SCAP** fiscal results.
- **A third-party aggregator's metric** vs audited disclosures.

Build a worksheet showing original report page and exact accounting-line name for every ratio. A calculation without an accounting definition and period is not verified.
