# 04 — Competitive advantages: hypotheses to test

**None of the following is established as a durable moat by this draft.** Each is a research hypothesis, not a reason to buy shares.

| Hypothesis | What would support it? | What would disprove it? |
|---|---|---|
| Brand recognition reduces acquisition cost | Consistently better acquisition cost per profitable customer | Rising marketing/commission spending without better cohort economics |
| Insurance distribution has scale benefits | Persistent growth, claims service quality, renewal/persistency and sustainable margins | Policy growth that does not translate into insurer value/profit |
| Cross-group distribution adds value | Documented profitable cross-selling and lower customer acquisition costs | Large related-party overhead with little attributable income |
| Brokerage research/client relationships support fee income | Repeat institutional clients, stable fees, differentiated service | Declining turnover share, falling fees and customer concentration |
| Asset-management expertise attracts sticky assets | Positive multi-year net flows, controlled cost ratio and repeatable risk-adjusted returns | Large redemptions, weak performance or fee compression |

## Framework

1. **Existence:** Is the claimed advantage real and measurable?
2. **Durability:** Has it persisted across economic cycles?
3. **Economics:** Does it improve margins, returns on capital or cash generation?
4. **Ownership:** How much value can actually reach SCAP shareholders?
5. **Valuation:** Even a good business can be expensive at a particular price.

## Findings at this stage

**[FACT — issuer description]** SCAP presents a diversified financial-services operating portfolio. [Company overview](https://softlogiccapital.lk/about-us-overview/).

**[OPEN]** This alone does not establish an economic advantage. Durable outperformance, unit economics, governance, dividend access and comparable financial results must be verified from multiple reporting periods.
