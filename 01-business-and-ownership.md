## What do shareholders own?

**[FACT — issuer description]** Softlogic Capital PLC (`SCAP.N0000`) was incorporated in April 2005 as Capital Reach Holdings Limited. Softlogic Holdings PLC acquired the company in August 2010. SCAP is the Softlogic Group's financial-services holding company. [Issuer overview](https://softlogiccapital.lk/about-us-overview/).

## Important: consolidated profit is not wholly yours

When SCAP consolidates a subsidiary it does not follow that every rupee of group profit belongs to SCAP shareholders. Review **profit attributable to owners of the parent**, **non-controlling interests**, and any shares pledged to lenders.

**[FACT — dated]** Softlogic Life's 2025 integrated annual report identifies Softlogic Capital PLC as owning **50.16%** of its shares **at 31 December 2025**. This is a dated ownership disclosure for the insurance company, **not** proof of the stake on a later trading date. [Softlogic Life Annual Report 2025, shareholder table](https://softlogiclife.lk/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2026/03/Softlogic-Life-Integrated-Annual-Report-2025.pdf). Review current CSE and issuer filings for changes.
