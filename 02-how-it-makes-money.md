# 02 — How SCAP makes money

**Research date: 2026-09-30 · Business model, not a verified segment profit forecast**

SCAP is a holding company; economic exposure comes from the operations it owns and the extent to which those businesses create earnings and distributable cash attributable to the parent.

## Life insurance

Softlogic Life sells life-protection and related insurance products. Important economic variables are sales and renewals of policies, policy persistency, claims/benefits, commissions, reinsurance, expenses, investments and regulatory solvency. **Gross written premium is not the same as accounting revenue or profit.** Examine the impact of SLFRS 17 and compare consistent periods.

- Profitability: insurance service result and investment result separately.
- Quality: protection mix, persistency, claims experience and customer-acquisition cost.
- Capital: solvency coverage, dividend restrictions and changes in embedded value where disclosed.
- Parent look-through: effective ownership and cash dividends distributable upstream.

Source: [Softlogic Life 2025 reporting portal](https://annualreport.softlogiclife.lk/).

## Non-bank finance

Softlogic Finance describes lending, leasing, gold-backed and other financial products alongside deposits. Basic unit economics: **interest/fee receipts – funding cost – expected credit losses – operating costs – tax**. Deposits are liabilities; total deposits are not revenue.

- Net interest margin, loan mix, credit quality and expected credit loss provisions.
- Deposit maturity profile, refinancing concentration, liquidity and capital.
- Extent of parent guarantees or required capital injections.

Source: [SCAP subsidiary descriptions](https://softlogiccapital.lk/subsidiaries/).

## Stockbroking

Potential earnings include commissions on executed transactions and other disclosed capital-markets services. Earnings may fluctuate with CSE market turnover, customer activity and commission pressure. **Do not assume the exact fee composition without the segment accounts.**

Source: [Softlogic Group — stockbrokers](https://www.softlogic.lk/financial-sector/company-details/16).

## Asset management

Unit trusts and managed funds can generate management/distribution fees linked to **assets under management × fee rates**. Assets held for customers are not automatically shareholder-owned assets. Look for AUM, redemption trends, net fees and cost ratios.

Source: [SCAP subsidiaries](https://softlogiccapital.lk/subsidiaries/).

## Key analytical question

How much value does each business contribute to **SCAP owners**, after minority interests, group eliminations, taxes, leverage, regulatory capital and holding-company costs?
