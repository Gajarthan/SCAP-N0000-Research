## Extracted facts

Printed p.56 states that during FY2025/26 Softlogic Capital progressed investment-portfolio restructuring, including the Softlogic Finance capital restoration programme; an identified financial-asset portfolio was transferred to **SR One (Pvt) Ltd**, described as a wholly-owned SPV established under a restructuring framework approved by CBSL. It reports SCAP **Company-level operating income LKR 1.5bn** and **total assets LKR 28.1bn**, up from **LKR 21.2bn**. For the broader Holdings **Financial Services sector**, revenue is **LKR 42.5bn** vs **32.4bn** (+31%), operating profit **LKR 5.5bn** (+38%), and PAT **LKR 3.7bn** (+25%). The report attributes improvement principally to life insurance and discusses Softlogic Finance rebuilding scale after normal operations resumed.
