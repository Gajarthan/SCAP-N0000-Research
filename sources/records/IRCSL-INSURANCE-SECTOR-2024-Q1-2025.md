# IRCSL-INSURANCE-SECTOR-2024-Q1-2025

- **Title:** Reviewing Sri Lanka's Insurance Sector Performance 2020–2024 and Q1 2025 (regulator press release)
- **Publisher / legal scope:** Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL); Sri Lankan life and general insurance **industry**, not Softlogic Life, SCAP or the SCAP consolidated group.
- **Canonical original:** https://ircsl.gov.lk/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PRESS-RELEASE-_-Reviewing-Sri-Lankas-Insurance-Sector-Performance-2020-to-2024-and-Q1-2025.pdf
- **Publication:** August 2025 (URL directory); exact issue day **not verified**. **Reporting periods:** calendar 2020–2024 provisional and quarter ended 31 March 2025. **Accessed:** 2026-09-30.
- **Assurance:** Regulatory industry statistical release, **not a company audit opinion**. **Original citations:** Chart 1, 'Gross Written Premium from 2020 to 2024'; accompanying text immediately after Chart 1 on Q1 2025 (PDF page number not independently confirmed).
- **Extracted facts:** Chart 1 reports **2024 provisional** life GWP LKR **183,875m**, general GWP **138,162m**, total **322,037m** (arithmetic 183,875 + 138,162 = 322,037). 2023 total GWP **277,186m**. Q1 2025 total GWP **92,229m** vs Q1 2024 **83,384m**. Life Q1 2025 **51,056m** vs Q1 2024 **42,058m**; general Q1 2025 **41,172m** vs **41,326m**. **Rounding:** 51,056 + 41,172 = 92,228m, **LKR 1m below** reported total; do not infer a restatement.
- **Contradictions / limitations:** Calendar industry GWP is **not** SCAP fiscal-year operating income, Softlogic Life company GWP or SCAP ordinary-shareholder profit. These data cannot establish insurer-specific market share, solvency or cash available for dividends. Later 2025/2026 industry handbooks and entity-specific filings require a separate dated comparison.
