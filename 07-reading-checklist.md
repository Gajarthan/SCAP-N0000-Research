# 07 — Checklist before buying SCAP shares

**Research date:** __________  **Price observed and time:** __________

## Corporate structure and reporting
- [ ] Read latest audited **consolidated** statements AND **SCAP company-only** statements.
- [ ] Read latest interim and material CSE announcements through today's date.
- [ ] Verify share count, parent ownership, minority interests and related-party exposures.
- [ ] Read auditor's opinion and significant judgments.

## Business economics
- [ ] Understand life insurance claims, persistency, investment result and regulatory solvency.
- [ ] Understand finance company credit losses, deposit stability and capital adequacy.
- [ ] Identify brokerage and asset-management recurring fees.
- [ ] Distinguish revenue growth from owner-attributable earnings and cash.

## Comparisons and risks
- [ ] Select relevant peers separately for insurance, lending and asset management.
- [ ] Check debt, pledges, group guarantees and cash that can be upstreamed.
- [ ] Verify current regulatory restrictions, if any, directly from disclosures.
- [ ] Quantify exposure to interest rates, credit quality and liquidity.

## Valuation and portfolio fit
- [ ] Collect current SCAP price/volume with source and time.
- [ ] Use attributable profits, the correct number of shares and a date-matched share price.
- [ ] Test valuation under more than one scenario and show sensitivity.
- [ ] Record risks, alternative explanations and conditions that would change the thesis.
- [ ] Consider transaction costs, portfolio concentration and ability to exit.
