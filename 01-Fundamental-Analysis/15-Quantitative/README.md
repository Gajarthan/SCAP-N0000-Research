## 🎯 Research question

Do statistically measured company fundamentals remain robust after accounting, period and data-quality adjustments?

## 🔎 What to verify

- [ ] Build a reproducible dataset of dated growth, credit-loss, solvency, return-on-equity and valuation measures.
- [ ] Check sample size, missing values, restatements, non-stationary accounting definitions and outlier one-off events.
- [ ] Use sensitivity checks, distributions and cross-company baselines without treating correlations as proof of causality.
