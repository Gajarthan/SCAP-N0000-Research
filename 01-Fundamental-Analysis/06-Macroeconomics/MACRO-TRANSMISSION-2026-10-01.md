### Transparent parent borrowing sensitivity — *illustration, not prediction*

Use the **LKR 17,324.26m FY2026 year-end interim parent borrowing balance** already recorded in the repository (verify against [SCAP FY2026 issuer interim, Company balance sheet](https://cdn.cse.lk/cmt/upload_report_file/1100_1779968696398.pdf)). Hold principal constant for 12 months and assume parallel rate repricing, without fees, taxes, principal payments or hedges:

| Share of borrowings repricing | +100 bp annual interest | +200 bp annual interest | −100 bp annual interest |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 25% | +LKR 43.31m | +LKR 86.62m | −LKR 43.31m |
| 50% | +LKR 86.62m | +LKR 173.24m | −LKR 86.62m |
| 100% | +LKR 173.24m | +LKR 346.49m | −LKR 173.24m |

Formula: borrowing balance × repricing share × annual rate change. A 100 bp change is 0.01, **not** 1.00. These values cannot be labelled FY2027 forecasts, interest-cover ratios or cash interest without tranche dates, reset mechanics and contractual spreads. Do not assume OPR moves one-for-one into commercial-paper yields.

### Parent versus regulated subsidiaries

SCAP Group cash, Life insurer cash and Finance depositor-funded liquidity are **not interchangeable**. A macro-driven increase in insurer profit or bond yields does not automatically become a dividend paid to SCAP. Trace regulatory capital, board declarations, record-date entitlement, taxes, lender collateral restrictions and parent bank receipts. Parent cash-flow reconciliation must use the standalone cash-flow statement, not dividend income alone.
