## 🎯 Research question

Can SCAP sustainably pay dividends to ordinary shareholders, rather than merely report consolidated profits?

## 🔎 What to verify

- [ ] Inspect five years of dividends per share, payment dates, share count and the ratio to parent-attributable profits.
- [ ] Follow dividends received by SCAP from subsidiaries, and review parent-only retained earnings, net cash and debt covenants.
- [ ] Check insurer/finance-company capital and regulatory restrictions before assuming upstream cash is available.

## ⚠️ What not to assume

A high historical dividend yield calculated from a past payment is not evidence of a future payout.

## 🛠️ SCAP application

Create a dated SCAP dividend timeline and an upstream cash-availability worksheet, distinguishing paid, declared and proposed distributions.
