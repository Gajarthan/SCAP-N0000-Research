# FY2026 governance transition — evidence note

**Research date:** 1 October 2026 · **Status: PARTIAL.** This note separates original issuer filings from secondary event reports and does not infer misconduct or a regulatory breach from board changes alone.

## Board and committee bridge

The original SCAP interim for the nine months ended **31 December 2025** lists five directors: A.K. Pathirage, R.J. Perera, V.S. Somasundaram, H. Kaimal and N.C.A. Abeysekera. Its Audit Committee and Related Party Transaction Review Committee each list Abeysekera as chair, with Somasundaram and Kaimal.

The original SCAP year-end interim at **31 March 2026** lists only Pathirage, Perera, Kaimal and Abeysekera. Somasundaram is absent. Audit and RPT Review Committees each list only Abeysekera as chair and Kaimal. A secondary S&P Capital IQ event report says Shanker Varadananda Somasunderam resigned as an Independent Non-Executive Director effective 31 March 2026. The original resignation notice remains OPEN.

## Related-party control test

An original CSE corporate disclosure dated **16 July 2025** states SCAP granted a **LKR 300m term loan** to wholly owned S R One (Private) Limited under an agreement dated 15 July 2025. The disclosure reports aggregate FY2026 transactions with that related party of **LKR 700m**, and aggregate non-recurrent related-party transactions for the financial year of **LKR 700m**. It states the RPT Review Committee considered the transaction to be on normal commercial terms and not prejudicial to SCAP or minority shareholders, after advice from independent experts.

That is evidence of a disclosed transaction and a stated committee review. It does **not** independently prove market pricing, repayment, collectability, cash settlement, expert assumptions or interested-director abstention.

## Disclosure-consistency finding

As reviewed on 1 October 2026, SCAP's dynamic board webpage still displays Somasundaram, while the March issuer interim omits him and later event reporting records his resignation. The webpage also does not reflect the June/July appointments reported by secondary event sources. Therefore the website should not be treated as an authoritative dated board register. This is not, by itself, evidence of a statutory disclosure violation.
