## 4. Related-party and lender-connected transactions — evidence protocol

The [SCAP FY2026 interim](https://cdn.cse.lk/cmt/upload_report_file/1100_1779968696398.pdf) has a related-party section reported in prior repository research as PDF **p.17**. The [FY2025 audited report](https://cdn.cse.lk/cmt/upload_report_file/1100_1764673838964.03.2025%20-%20Annual%20Report.pdf) is a separate historical source. Build an entity-by-entity ledger for transactions among SCAP standalone, Holdings, Life, Finance, directors/KMP, and lender-related entities. For each transaction capture: legal counterparty, director connection, amount and currency, financial-statement note, transaction versus outstanding balance, interest/security terms, approval body/date, abstention evidence, and settlement/cash flow. **Do not conflate group eliminations with parent-only cash receipts or assume a related-party transaction is improper.**

The issuer biography mentions Ashok Pathirage's NDB role; the repository's FY2025 audit evidence separately describes NDB/DFCC pledges of Life shares. These **different-dated facts warrant a documented conflict-management check**, not an allegation of an undisclosed conflict or improper lending. Confirm contemporaneous appointments, recusal requirements, lending terms and security-release dates before any conclusion.
