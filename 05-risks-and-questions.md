# 05 — Business risks and unanswered questions

This is a risk inventory, **not a prediction of failure**.

| Risk | Why it matters | Evidence to obtain |
|---|---|---|
| Holding-company leverage | Debt service may consume cash before ordinary dividends | SCAP company-only borrowing, guarantees, pledges, maturities |
| Non-controlling interests | Subsidiary profits do not all belong to SCAP owners | Ownership table, consolidated attribution note |
| Insurer underwriting/investments | Claims, customer retention, interest rates and investments can affect value | Insurance service results, investment result, solvency returns |
| Finance credit/funding | Bad loans, deposit outflows or costly refinancing can weaken a lender | Stage-3/NPL, impairment, liquidity and regulatory capital |
| Related-party exposure | Intra-group support can transfer risk or constrain cash | Related-party notes, receivables, security and guarantees |
| Regulatory change | Capital/licence/distribution restrictions can alter earnings and dividends | CBSL, IRCSL and SEC announcements, issuer filings |
| Interest rates and currency | Can change asset yields, funding cost and investments | Asset/liability sensitivity, investment composition |
| Trading liquidity | Small turnover can make exits costly and prices volatile | Dated bid/ask, free float, traded volume and depth |

**Historical context only:** EconomyNext reported that regulatory restrictions on Softlogic Finance were lifted **effective 19 September 2025**, after a capital-restoration process. This is not proof that the business is risk-free today. [News account](https://economynext.com/sri-lankas-softlogic-finance-to-resume-business-after-central-bank-lifts-restrictions-241534/). Verify the original CSE/CBSL notice and later developments.

## Priority diligence questions

- [ ] What is the auditor's opinion for the latest SCAP audited consolidated and company-only accounts?
- [ ] What is the profit/loss **attributable to owners of the parent**, including extraordinary items?
- [ ] What is the current ownership % in Softlogic Life, Softlogic Finance and other businesses?
- [ ] Which operations have material debt and where does security sit?
- [ ] Have there been capital increases, disposals, share pledges or rights issues?
- [ ] Are subsidiary dividends legally available and practically paid to SCAP?
- [ ] Are recent headline earnings backed by operating results and cash?
- [ ] What has changed since the most recent financial reporting date?

Document every answer with a **document, date, page or financial-statement note**.
