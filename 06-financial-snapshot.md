## Group ≠ owners ≠ company-only

**Reconciliation:** group PAT is **LKR 1,694.15mn (FY25 audited comparison)** and **LKR 3,371.26mn (FY26 interim)**, but SCAP owners' share is the smaller row above (FY25 **loss LKR 280.42mn**, FY26 **profit LKR 973.77mn**) because non-controlling interests receive the rest. Parent-only FY26 loss is **LKR 722.49mn**; parent interest-bearing debt **17,324.26mn**, overdraft **323.13mn**, cash/bank **32.07mn**. These are *not* the same as group income or group borrowings. **[CSE PDF pp. 2–3, 6, 9](https://cdn.cse.lk/cmt/upload_report_file/1100_1779968696398.pdf)**.
